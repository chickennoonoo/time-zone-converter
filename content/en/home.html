How {{SITE_NAME}} works

{{SITE_NAME}} is a free time zone converter. Pick the place and time you are converting from, and every place under "Converting to" shows the matching local time, including the day difference and daylight saving changes.

  • Set the time: drag the slider, or click the big time (or the box under it) to type an exact time.
  • Change where you're converting from: click the name at the top to choose any country, city or time zone.
  • Add places to compare: use the Add a place or time zone button to compare as many places as you need, and remove the ones you don't.
  • Convert time zones, not just cities: search for PST, EST, GMT, CET and other time zone names. Pick "PT" or "ET" for Pacific or Eastern time as it is observed right now, including daylight saving, or "PST" or "EDT" for the exact fixed offset.
  • 12-hour or 24-hour: switch the format with the 12H / 24H toggle at the top.
