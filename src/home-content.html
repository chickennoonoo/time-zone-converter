How {{SITE_NAME}} works

{{SITE_NAME}} is a free time zone converter. Pick the place and time you are starting from, and every destination below it shows the matching local time, including the day difference and daylight saving changes.

  • Set the time: drag the slider, or click the big time (or the box under it) to type an exact time.
  • Change the starting place: click the city name at the top to choose any country or city.
  • Add destinations: use the Add Destination button to compare as many places as you need, and remove the ones you don't.
  • 12-hour or 24-hour: switch the format with the 12H / 24H toggle at the top.
